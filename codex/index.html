How the allocation is calculated

The calculator follows the same sequence each time. Eligible-faculty counts determine proportional shares; applicant counts only limit how many of those seats a college can actually use.

  1. 1
    Determine the total seat pool

    Add eligible faculty across all colleges and take 12%. Any fractional result is rounded up to the next whole seat. For example, 101 eligible faculty produces 12.12, which becomes 13 seats.

  2. 2
    Calculate each exact Hamilton quota

    In round one, every college participates—even a college with no applicants. Its exact proportional quota is:

    round pool × college eligible ÷ participating eligible
  3. 3
    Award the whole-number portions

    Each college first receives the whole-number part (the floor) of its exact quota. These floors are added to find how many seats remain in the round.

  4. 4
    Award the largest remainders and resolve ties

    The remaining seats go one at a time to the largest fractional quota parts. For equal fractions, the larger current carry-forward wins first; remaining ties use more eligible faculty, then college name A–Z (case-insensitive), then original entry order.

  5. 5
    Apply the applicant limit

    A college can use no more seats than it has applicants not yet funded. Any portion of its Hamilton offer above that limit is returned to the seat pool.

  6. 6
    Redistribute returned seats

    Each later round repeats the Hamilton calculation using the returned pool, but only colleges with unfunded applicants participate. Their eligible-faculty counts remain the proportional weights.

  7. 7
    Stop and report the outcome

    The process stops when no seats remain or no college has an unfunded applicant. Any seats left are unallocated. Prior carry-forward persists until a college wins a cutoff tie, when it clears. A tied loser with unmet demand and no prior value carries that tied fraction into next year.

How to verify a result: After calculation, open any round in the audit trail. It shows the exact quota arithmetic, floor, fractional part, remainder priority, Hamilton offer, applicants before the round, seats funded, seats returned, and applicants still unfunded.
